# Planora 1.8.12

## 中文

- Mac 任务列表双击任务或使用右键「任务详情」，现在直接进入完整详情页，不再打开弹窗或右侧任务检查器。
- 首页和任务列表复用同一个详情页面。宽窗口中的内容居中并限制阅读宽度，避免字段被拉到两端；窄窗口保持自适应。
- 单击任务仅选择列表行，保留多选和批量操作。返回后可以再次打开同一任务；置顶和编辑由当前详情页提供。
- 无需重置或重新导入数据。未完成的引导页工作不包含在此次更新中。

## English

- Double-click a task or choose Task Details from its context menu to navigate to a full-page detail view on Mac. Remove the task inspector and detail sheet.
- Home and Tasks share the same detail view, with centered, width-limited content on wide windows and adaptive layout on narrow windows.
- Single-click selects rows without opening an inspector. Multi-selection and bulk actions remain available; detail pages own their Pin and Edit controls.
- No data reset or reimport is required.
