# Planora 1.8.1

## 中文

- Mac 常驻菜单栏图标采用应用图标的交错圆角任务条轮廓，去除背景并跟随系统深浅配色。
- 未完成任务在截止前 24 小时及逾期时自动显示高优先级；详情标注“自动”，保留原手动优先级，不修改学校端数据。
- iOS、iPadOS 与 macOS 的任务详情可点击课程行进入相应课程。

- 同步完成标题改为快速/完整同步完成，摘要显示本次读取课程、单元、消息及课次，不再混用本地课程与单元总数。
- 快速同步仅显示实际执行的 7 项步骤；单元显示“未重新扫描”，不再暗示已读取全部内容。
- 已有任务计数改为“刷新已有任务”，避免将每次重新读取都描述成内容发生变化。
- 出勤区分“未读取”和“未记录”，不再显示误导性的零；标签明确为本次同步周出勤，区别于首页最近 7 天。
- 连接页增加同步方式与上次完整同步时间，更新中英日文案。

Mac 包为临时签名，未经过 Apple 公证。真实账户同步和实际旧版升级仍需用户验证。

## English

- The Mac menu-bar template icon uses Planora's alternating rounded task bars without a background and follows system appearance.
- Incomplete tasks become visually high priority within 24 hours of their deadline or when overdue. Details identify automatic priority; manual values and school data remain unchanged.
- Task details link directly to the associated course on iOS, iPadOS and macOS.

- Completion titles distinguish quick and full sync. Results show records read in this scan rather than local course/unit totals.
- Quick sync shows its seven executed steps; units are marked Not Rescanned.
- Existing task counts now say Tasks Refreshed rather than implying every reread changed the content.
- Attendance distinguishes Not Read from Not Recorded instead of displaying zero. The label identifies the synced week, separate from Home's seven-day window.
- Connection details show the latest sync mode and last full sync time. Chinese, English and Japanese labels are updated.

The Mac package is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. Real-account syncing and real-device upgrades still require user verification.
