# Planora 1.8.9

## 中文

- 菜单栏打开任务或同步时，等应用激活且主窗口成为活动窗口后再接收路由；不再重复设置相同的激活策略，不使用固定延时。
- 菜单栏任务逐条显示优先级，自动升高时沿用自动优先级文案；底部时间明确标为“上次同步”。
- 任务检查器的置顶按钮独立居中；编辑并入主窗口的通知/头像按钮组。单独任务窗口仍保留自己的编辑入口。
- 任务页同步、详情及操作按钮使用一块局部玻璃板，增加与状态切换栏之间的间距。没有设置全局玻璃按钮样式。
- Vlog 本地版本保留开发者编辑入口的可见性规则；不发布 Vlog。

## English

- Defer menu-bar task/sync routes until the app is active and the main window is key. Avoid redundant activation-policy changes; do not add fixed delays.
- Show each task's priority and label the footer timestamp as Last Sync.
- Center the inspector pin and group Edit with notification/profile controls. Standalone task details retain their own editing entry.
- Apply a local glass surface to workspace sync/detail/action controls with additional separation from the status picker.
- Preserve Vlog developer-edit visibility rules; Vlog is not distributed.

## Verification

- Task-route and task-layout regression tests; production Mac/iOS and local Vlog builds.
- The supplied recording path was no longer present, so the reported flashing frames could not be inspected. Activation sequencing is corrected in code, but elimination of the observed flicker needs confirmation from the recording or the installed build.
- This Mac distribution is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized.
