# Planora 1.8.5

## 中文

- 首次启动与引导使用当前 Icon Composer 图标的真实浅色、深色渲染，替换旧 P 字母标识。
- 冷白与石墨灰背景、克制的蓝绿强调色，柔和的显现与过渡，不增加已完成引导用户的启动等待。
- 介绍页更新为任务计划、课程成绩及最近七天出勤和消息；可滚动内容与底部开始按钮适配小屏及大字体。
- 网页演示同步更新图标、深浅色与首次启动设计。原有 Entrance Film 源码保持不变。

Mac 安装包使用临时签名，未经过 Apple 公证。

## English

- First-launch welcome and introduction now use actual light/dark renders of the current Icon Composer artwork instead of the old P mark.
- Cool-white and graphite surfaces, restrained blue-green accents and gentle transitions. Returning users still open Home directly.
- The introduction now covers task planning, courses/grades and recent attendance/messages. Scrollable content and a bottom action accommodate small screens and larger text.
- The browser demo matches the updated artwork, appearances and first-launch design. Existing Entrance Film source is unchanged.

The Mac package is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized.
