# Planora 1.7.6

## 中文

- 修正 Mac 侧栏图标的布局方式，针对首次启动及台前调度切回应用时图标消失的问题，改为独立、固定尺寸的图标行。
- 修复 ManageBac 截止时间被日期整理代码重置为凌晨 12:00 的问题，保留导入的时分和时区对应的时间点。更新后请同步一次 ManageBac，恢复此前已被重置的截止时间。
- 提醒、重复和批量操作改用 Mac 原生分组表单，内容从顶部排列，修正大片空白和控件挤在中央的问题。
- DMG 继续使用 Finder 原生背景设置，图片保存在映像内部的 `.background/installation.png`，并保留中英文安装说明。
- 保留 Mac 专用的 Vendor/Sparkle 更新框架，提供签名更新归档和 appcast。iPhone/iPad 不包含 Sparkle。

验证范围：Mac 通用版本及 iOS Simulator 构建、截止时间针对性回归测试、DMG 内容和更新签名检查。台前调度和新设备的最终显示效果由用户验证，本次不进行长时间真机测试。

Mac 应用使用临时签名，未经过 Apple Developer ID 签名或公证；Sparkle 更新签名用于验证更新来源。

## English

- Replaced automatic sidebar label icons with independently laid-out, fixed-size symbols to address missing icons at first launch and when returning through Stage Manager.
- Fixed date normalization resetting ManageBac deadlines to midnight. Imported times now retain their exact timestamp across time zones. Sync ManageBac once after updating to restore previously reset deadlines.
- Switched reminder, recurrence, and bulk-action editors to native Mac grouped forms with top-aligned content.
- Retained Finder-native DMG background settings and the embedded `.background/installation.png`, with bilingual installation instructions.
- Retained the Mac-only vendored Sparkle framework, signed update archive, and appcast. Sparkle is not included in iPhone/iPad builds.

Validation covers the universal Mac and iOS Simulator builds, focused deadline regression tests, DMG contents, and update signatures. Final Stage Manager and new-device appearance checks are left to the user; no extended physical-device testing was performed.

The Mac app is ad-hoc signed, without Apple Developer ID signing or notarization. Sparkle signatures verify the update source.
